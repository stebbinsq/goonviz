Show checklist

  1. Power the MPD32 and load preset 18 ArkaosVJ before starting GoonViz.
  2. Pads latch in that preset: every hit counts, so ignore the pad lights.
  3. Bring MASTER (F8) up. Keep a finger near BLACKOUT (S7) or ■ STOP.
  4. Autopilot (S1 / ▶ PLAY) switches presets and keeps every knob moving. Bank B F7 sets intensity, from calm to wild. Any knob you touch, autopilot leaves alone until the next switch.
  5. Save looks: S8 SHIFT on, hit a blue snapshot pad, SHIFT off.

If something's off

  • Pads do nothing: on the Pi run run.py --learn-pads and hit the pads in order.
  • Change any control: plug in a keyboard, H, ↑↓ to choose, L, move the control.
  • Then reprint: python tools/make_cheatsheet.py
  • No reaction to music: check the audio input; Audio react is bank B K5.
